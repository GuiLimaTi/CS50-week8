My Journey

My career started in electrical engineering in Brazil, working on installation, maintenance and upgrade projects for homes and small businesses, and later running my own electrical and CCTV business.

Installing CCTV systems and configuring networks for remote access brought me closer to IT, so I started learning programming and core computing concepts.

I built my foundations through Harvard's CS50 and personal projects. In 2024 I moved to Dublin, where I am now studying for a Higher Diploma in Science in Computing at the National College of Ireland.

Today my focus is IT support: troubleshooting, Windows, Active Directory and networking, with a long-term goal of working in cybersecurity.
